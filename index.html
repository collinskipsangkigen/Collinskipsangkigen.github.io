About the author

Education, teaching and storytelling.

I am a Kenyan teacher and educator with an academic background in Mathematics and Geography. Alongside teaching, I have developed a strong interest in literature and creative writing.

My work brings together education and storytelling, with a focus on ideas, relationships, society and the experiences that shape people's lives.

“Education gives us knowledge; stories help us understand the people behind that knowledge.”
