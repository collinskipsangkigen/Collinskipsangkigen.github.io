ABOUT ME

The person behind the pages.

I believe stories begin with the people, places and conversations that stay with us.

Teacher in Kenya · Author · Storyteller

As a teacher in Kenya and a writer, I am interested in people, relationships and the experiences that shape the communities around us. Writing When Silence Breaks gave me an opportunity to turn ideas about family, relationships, trust and difficult choices into a story readers can enter and experience.

My aim is simple: to tell a meaningful story, connect with readers and leave them with something to think about long after they have closed the book.

MY WORLD

Rooted in my environment.

When Silence Breaks is presented as a story shaped by Kenyan life, family, community and cultural surroundings.

The setting and characters give the novel a sense of place. The story moves through homes, gardens, village life and community relationships, bringing the reader into an environment where ordinary life and hidden struggles meet.

This is the world behind the story—the people, conversations, relationships and experiences that make the novel feel human.
